# Problem Statement

The rapid growth of Artificial Intelligence and Data-related roles has created a highly competitive and dynamic job market. However, both job seekers and hiring organizations face challenges in understanding fair compensation trends across industries, experience levels, company sizes, locations, and work arrangements (remote vs on-site).

Many professionals struggle to determine:
- Which industries offer the highest-paying AI and Data roles
- How experience level and company size impact salary expectations
- Whether geographic location still plays a major role in compensation
- How remote work influences salary equality across regions

At the same time, organizations lack clear, data-driven insights to benchmark salaries competitively and design attractive compensation strategies.

This project aims to address these gaps by analyzing real-world AI and Data job salary data using SQL for data transformation and Tableau for visualization. The goal is to uncover actionable insights that support informed career decisions for job seekers and strategic compensation planning for businesses.
