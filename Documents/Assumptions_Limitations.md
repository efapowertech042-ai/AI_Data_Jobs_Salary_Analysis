# Assumptions and Limitations

While this analysis provides valuable insights into AI and Data job salary trends, it is based on several assumptions and has inherent limitations that should be considered when interpreting the results.

## Assumptions
- Salary ranges accurately represent total annual compensation and exclude bonuses, stock options, or other benefits unless explicitly stated.
- The midpoint of salary ranges is a reasonable proxy for average salary.
- Job postings reflect current market conditions at the time of data collection.
- Industry and company size classifications are consistent and comparable across records.
- Remote, hybrid, and on-site classifications are correctly labeled in the dataset.

## Limitations
- The dataset may not fully represent all global job markets, particularly emerging economies.
- Some industries or experience levels may be underrepresented, which can affect average salary calculations.
- Salary data is based on job postings rather than confirmed offers or actual employee compensation.
- Currency conversions and regional cost-of-living differences are not explicitly accounted for.
- Temporal trends may be influenced by external economic factors not captured in the dataset.

## Interpretation Guidance
The insights and recommendations from this project should be used as directional guidance rather than absolute benchmarks. Stakeholders are encouraged to combine these findings with additional market research and contextual knowledge when making career or business decisions.
